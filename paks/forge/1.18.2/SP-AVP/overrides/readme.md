# SquirrelPAK AVP

SquirrelPAK Almost Vanilla PLUS!

Say what! My fellow squirrels look out, SquirrelCraft's release of its second in a line of forge mod packs for 1.18 has now hit forge.

In this release, SquirrelPAK AV+ Almost Vanilla PLUS! is Vanilla Minecraft PLUS a barrel of squirrels. Well ok not really, but what it does have is mods that we here at SquirrelHQ think will be a good base for our upcoming 1.18.2 +Plus mod PAKs. We took our "Almost Vanilla" PAK and gave it a good sprinkle of mods. The squirrels here at SquirrelCraftHQ do not have a lot of time to play this awesome game called Minecraft, so we added mods and tweaked config to maximize time put in to game satisfaction out. 

This PAK will be the base PAK for our +Plus serials of mod PAKs where we pick 2-3 main mods or pick a theme of mods, keep an eye out for those releases. 

Download SquirrelPAK Almost Vanilla PLUS! today and see what happens when you take Minecraft add some squirrels then ended it with a BANG!
