# SquirrelPAK JMK

SquirrelPAK Just.. MeKanism


Hello fellow crafting squirrels, welcome to the first installment of "The Just.." series of mods from SquirrelCraft HQ. 

In this series we highlight a single mod or mod suite of mods. We start with our AVP or TEC mod pack then add just one mod/mod suite. In this installment we have "Just.. MeKanism (JMK)"! 

We started with our Almost Vanilla Plus! (AVP) mod pack then added the Mekanism suite of mods then a dash of Mekanism related mods. Because JMK is based off of our AVP pack you get all the SquirrelPAK life improvement mods you come to know and love (AV PAK) PLUS mods to help early game play to get you off and running with Mekanism. You will be building a fusion reactor in no time, well maybe not. We also added Better Fusion Reactor for Mekanism mod to make fusion reactors just a bit more should I say, realistic ;-)

 
Download SquirrelCraft - SquirrelPAK JMK, Just.. MeKanism today and explore the Mekanism mod the SquirrelCraft way!
