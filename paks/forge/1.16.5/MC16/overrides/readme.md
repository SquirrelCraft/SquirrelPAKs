# SquirrelPAK MC16


> TL;DR: SquirrelCraft SquirrelPAK Creative Adventure is medium tech mod pack focused around four main mods: Create, Immersive Engineering/Petroleum, and PneumaticCraft. With a heavy dose of building mods that will make your base look amazing, and the quality of life mods you come to know.

 
### Welcome back fellow Squirrels!

Ever since I started working on MC12, MC16 was always on my mind. This time, thought we would shake it up a bit. With all talk these days around the Create mod and so much you can do with it I thought this would be a great mod to build our next 1.16 mod pack around.

After some research, I felt since Create seems to be a mid-tech mod we would focus on mods that are low/med tech with maybe one mod being hitech for those who like that kind of thing. So what to expect from this PAK? Well for starters the pack is built around four main mods. Create Immersive Engineering/Petroleum, and PneumaticCraft. This brings a nice mixture of low/med tech mods and the use of PneumaticCraft for the hi tech mod.

 

Like the MC12 PAK we have Pam's Harvest Craft for the foodies out there along with Cooking for Block Heads, along with Macaws series of mods that make your base and building look stunning! A change in MC16 is additional world generation mods that will allow for awesome world exploring such as Oh The Biomes You will Go will spruce up your world generation exploration experience! For you adventurers out there we have added a lot! With DungeonCrawl, Castle Dungeons, and Better Strongholds for more things to do while you stock up on resources. For those animal lovers we have included Better Animals Plus, and Wings Horns & Hooves to keep you company. These mods add more animals, birds, and Unicorns! Also included are the quality of life mods like JIE, Mouse Tweeks, ReAuth, and FTB mods that will help with server administration.

 

A bonus mod to make it better for all is the addition of Village Spawnpoint, a mod that will always set world spawn in a village! So all this sound good? Ready to start creating cool factories with Create, build industrial base with Immersive Engineering, or go hitech with PneumaticCraft? Well down load and start playing! Oh by the way there may be things hiding, just a warning there be dragons, really!
