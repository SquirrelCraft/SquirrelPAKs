# SquirrelPAK 123

Welcome to Team SquirrelCraft's first ever PAK on one of the newest version of Mincraft! 

> tl;dr: 123 Easy Squirrels! - A 1.21.11 mod pack by Team SquirrelCraft that is aimed for the player that has limited time to play on the newest MC version

### Whats a SquirrelPAK?
Welcome to Team SquirrelCraft's first SquirrelPAK on Modrinth! If your new to SquirrelPAKs they are mod packs made for folks who don't have a lot of time to play and may short attention spans! With this in mind we try to select mods and settings that make it little easier to get started and progress quickly.

### About This PAK
SquirrelPAK 123 Easy Squirrels is our first PAK on NeoForge launcher and our first up to date Minecraft version! We have out standard mods that we can't live with out, then added quality of life mods and just a hint of tech mods! See the mod list for full list. Playing this PAK is as Easy as 1 2 3!
